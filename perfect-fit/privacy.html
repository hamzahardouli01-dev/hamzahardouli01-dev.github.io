Perfect Fit: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Perfect Fit ("the game") is made by Hamza Hardouli. This policy explains what data is involved when you play.

What the game itself keeps

Your progress, stars, badges and settings are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. They are never sent to us. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; the Android Advertising ID on Android), plus an app-specific identifier.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address.
  • Interactions with ads: for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped.
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance data from the ads SDK.

Google's use of this data is described in its policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the first time you open the game, you are asked whether ads may use your data, through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy.
  • Tracking on iPhone: the game asks for permission before it uses the advertising identifier. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised by tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads, or in Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Data sale

We do not sell your personal data. We have no servers that receive or store it.

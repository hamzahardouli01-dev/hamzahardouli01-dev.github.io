Perfect Fit: Carpenter Puzzle

Measure, mark and cut the last floor board. A woodworking puzzle game for iPhone and iPad.

Support

Questions, a bug, or a job you can't crack? Email contact@lixuslink.com. Include your device model and the job number, and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. There's no account, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I change my ad privacy choice? Open Settings in the game and tap Privacy. On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How do I see the tool guide again? Open Settings in the game and tap How to play.
